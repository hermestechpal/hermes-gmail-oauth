Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Hermes Agent Gmail accesses and handles data through its connection to Gmail.

OAuth Access

Gmail access is granted and controlled exclusively through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. No credentials are collected or stored outside of this flow.

Scope of Use

The application reads and sends email only as explicitly authorized by the account owner through the OAuth consent screen, and only for the scopes granted at that time.

Purpose of Data Processing

Email data accessed through this integration is processed solely to support the owner's personal automation and assistant tasks. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's own workflows.

Credential Storage

OAuth credentials (tokens and client secrets) are stored privately on the owner's own server infrastructure. They are never committed to this repository or any public location.

No Publication or Sale of Data

Email contents and OAuth credentials are not intentionally published, shared, or sold to any third party.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke this application's access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
