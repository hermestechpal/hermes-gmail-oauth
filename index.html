Hermes Agent Gmail

Hermes Agent Gmail is a personal Gmail integration used by its owner to connect a single Gmail account to automated personal assistant workflows.

It uses Google's OAuth 2.0 flow to read and send email on behalf of the account owner only, strictly within the scopes the owner has explicitly authorized. It is not a public or multi-tenant service, and it is not intended for use by anyone other than its owner.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
